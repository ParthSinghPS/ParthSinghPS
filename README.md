## About me

I am a blockchain developer increasingly focused on the systems beneath Web3: execution clients, data structures, serialization, interoperability, and open source protocol infrastructure.

My work and experience span three layers:

```text
Ethereum protocol research and client development
                       ↓
Cross chain infrastructure and interoperability
                       ↓
Smart contract and Web3 application experience
```

I enjoy understanding how protocols work internally, turning that understanding into maintainable code, and making complex systems easier for contributors to navigate.

### ⚙️ Pureth in Reth

**Permissionless Fellow | Ethereum Protocol Fellowship**

Working on experimental SSZ native execution layer data in a Reth fork, with an initial focus on withdrawals, receipts, logs, deterministic roots, and proof friendly representations.

The broader goal is to make Ethereum execution data easier to access, verify, and consume without depending entirely on trusted RPC providers or centralized indexers.
